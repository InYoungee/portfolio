About

Hi, I'm Inyoung—a Localization Program Manager with 6+ years of experience building the scalable systems that help global products feel genuinely local, not just translated.

I believe great localization lives at the intersection of storytelling, cultural intelligence, and operational precision. I'm a story lover by nature, which is what brought me to this work—and what keeps me obsessed with getting it right for every audience, in every language.

Outside of work, I build Python and ML tools for localization—LLM evaluator, MT quality predictors, capacity forecasting models, and workflow automation—because I think data should inform every localization decision, and I wanted to build the tools to make that possible.
